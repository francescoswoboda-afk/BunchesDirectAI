Privacy Policy

What personal data Bunches Direct collects, why we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Who We Are

Bunches Direct imports premium roses from Ecuador and supplies them to business customers across Europe. Bunches Direct is responsible (the “controller”) for the personal data described on this page. For any privacy question or request, email es@bunches-direct.com.

What We Collect

We only collect what you send us through the website: company name, contact person, company email, phone or WhatsApp number, delivery address, tax or VAT number, truck company in Aalsmeer, requested delivery date, the roses and boxes you order, and any message you write to us. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

Why We Use It and On What Legal Basis

Answering enquiries sent through the contact form, to take steps you asked for before an order (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

Handling orders: preparing the order document, sending confirmation emails, arranging delivery and invoicing, to perform our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b)).

Checking VAT numbers through the European Commission’s VIES service and keeping business records, because tax law requires it (Article 6(1)(c)).

Customer service and keeping the website secure, based on our legitimate interest in running the business properly (Article 6(1)(f)).

Who Receives Your Data

We never sell your data. We share it only with the services we need to run the website and process orders:

  • Google (Gmail) – sends order and confirmation emails.
  • FormSubmit – delivers messages from the contact form to our inbox.
  • Stripe – processes card payments, when you choose to pay by card.
  • European Commission (VIES) – confirms that a VAT number is valid.
  • The truck company you choose, to deliver your order.

Google, FormSubmit and Stripe may process data in the United States. Where they do, the transfer relies on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How Long We Keep It

Order details, invoices and related correspondence are kept for 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires. Contact form messages that do not lead to an order are deleted within 2 years. Your cart and order details saved in your own browser stay there until you place the order or clear your browser data.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. Email es@bunches-direct.com and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the authority in your own country.

Changes

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
