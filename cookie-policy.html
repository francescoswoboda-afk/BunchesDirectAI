Cookie Policy

What Bunches Direct stores on your device, and why.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

The Short Version

Bunches Direct does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. The only thing we store on your device is what the shop needs to work, so we do not need to ask for your consent.

What We Store in Your Browser

We use your browser’s local storage (not cookies) for three things:

  • bunchesDirectCart – the roses and boxes in your cart.
  • bunchesDirectOrderDetails – the delivery details you enter, so you don’t lose them while moving through checkout.
  • bunchesDirectCookieConsent – remembers that you closed the cookie notice.

This information stays on your device. It is only sent to us when you place an order or send the contact form.

Fonts and Other Files

Our fonts and images are served from our own website, so loading a page does not contact Google or other third parties.

Third-Party Services

If you pay by card you are taken to Stripe’s secure payment page, which may set its own cookies under Stripe’s cookie policy. The contact form is sent through our own website and sets no cookies.

Removing Stored Data

You can delete this data at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for bunches-direct.com. Your cart and saved delivery details will then be emptied.

Changes

If we ever add other cookies or tools, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent first.
