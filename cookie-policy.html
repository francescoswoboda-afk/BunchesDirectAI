Cookie Policy

This page explains how Bunches Direct uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies on the website.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, including local storage, can also be used to remember your activity or preferences.

How We Use Them

Bunches Direct currently uses browser storage to support essential shopping and ordering features, such as saving cart contents and temporarily retaining order details while you move through the checkout flow.

Essential Website Storage

Some storage is necessary for the website to function correctly. Without it, product selections, checkout details, or order progress may not work as expected.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services such as Stripe or FormSubmit may place their own cookies or related technologies when you use payment or contact form features. Their cookie practices are governed by their own policies.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies and site data through your browser settings. You may delete stored data or block cookies, but parts of the site may then work incorrectly or stop functioning during checkout.

Updates

We may update this policy if website functionality or third-party tools change.
